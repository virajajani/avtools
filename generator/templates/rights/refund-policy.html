At AVTools, we strive to provide reliable digital tools for our users. This policy outlines how cancellations and refunds are handled for all our services.

1. Nature of Services

AVTools provides 100% digital services — including AI image generation, image processing, label cropping, and utility tools for Meesho and other Indian e-commerce sellers. All services are delivered instantly and digitally upon usage. No physical goods are shipped or delivered at any time.

2. Cancellation Policy

⏱ Cancellation Window: 1 Hour from Purchase
Cancellation requests are only accepted within 1 hour of the original purchase, and only if zero credits have been consumed. Once even one credit is used, cancellation is not possible for that purchase.

Cancellation Scenario Eligible? Time Limit Outcome

Credit pack purchased — any credits used ❌ No — Not eligible; used credits are consumed upon service delivery
Cancellation request after 1 hour ❌ No Window expired Not accepted regardless of unused credit balance
Duplicate / double payment for same order ✅ Yes Within 7 days Duplicate amount refunded within 5–7 business days after verification
Payment deducted — credits not credited (transaction failure) ✅ Yes (Full Refund) Within 7 days Full refund within 5–7 business days after confirmation

How to Cancel: Email avtools.in@gmail.com with subject line: "Cancellation Request – [Order ID / Registered Email]". Include your purchase date and reason. We acknowledge requests within 2 business days.

4. Refund Mode

All eligible refunds are processed to the original payment source — UPI ID, Debit/Credit Card, or Net Banking account — used at the time of purchase. Refunds are initiated through PayU India and are subject to your bank's processing schedule (typically 3–7 additional business days after initiation). We do not issue refunds in cash, by cheque, or via any third-party wallet.

{% endcomment %}

4. Shipping Policy

📦 AVTools is a fully digital platform. We do not ship any physical products. There is no courier, no postal delivery, and no physical address required for service delivery.

All service outputs — generated images, processed label files, and account credits — are delivered digitally and instantly:

  • AI-generated images are available for immediate browser download upon generation.
  • Cropped label files are ready for download within seconds of upload and processing.
  • Credit packs are activated instantly in your account dashboard upon successful payment confirmation.
  • In the rare event of a technical delay exceeding 24 hours, you are entitled to a full refund under Section 3 of this policy.

Since all services are digital, there are no shipping charges, no delivery timelines, and no physical address requirements associated with any AVTools purchase.

5. How to Request a Refund or Cancellation

  • Email avtools.in@gmail.com with subject: "Refund Request – [Your Email / Order ID]" or "Cancellation Request – [Your Email / Order ID]"
  • Include your registered email address, the date of transaction, PayU transaction/order ID, and your reason.
  • Attach a screenshot of your payment confirmation if available.
  • We will acknowledge your request within 2 business days.
  • After verification, eligible refunds are processed within 5–10 business days and credited via the payment gateway to your original payment method.

6. Dispute Resolution

If you believe a transaction has been incorrectly charged or a refund has not been processed, contact us at avtools.in@gmail.com. We aim to resolve all disputes within 10 business days. If unresolved, disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in India.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy at any time without prior notice. Continued use of our services means acceptance of the updated policy. The latest version is always available at avtools.in/refund-policy.
