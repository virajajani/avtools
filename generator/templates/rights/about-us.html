What We Do

Our core product is an AI-powered image optimization platform for online sellers. Here's the problem we solve: when you create a product catalogue on Meesho or similar platforms, the shipping charge is calculated based on the weight slab your product falls into — and a large, heavy image file can push your product into a higher (more expensive) shipping slab.

With AVTools, sellers can upload their product images and our AI automatically optimizes, resizes, and processes them so the final image fits within a lower shipping weight slab — directly reducing your per-order shipping cost without any compromise on visual quality.

💡 Simple example: A seller listing a saree on Meesho may be paying ₹60/order in shipping because their catalogue image pushes the product into a higher slab. After processing through AVTools, the same listing can qualify for a ₹35/order slab — saving ₹25 per order. At 100 orders/month, that's ₹2,500 saved every month from one simple change.

Our Mission
